# Skill Routing and Consent

After simple-question isolation, Hive loads at most one narrowly matching approved Skill. Optional third-party or generated Skills require explicit preview and user approval before activation.

For the requested release acceptance, user-provided fresh Windows/Codex replies confirm explicit and natural selection of project-local ship, with preserved checks and new commit separation rules. This read-only evidence does not prove test, Unity or commit execution, or every future selection.
